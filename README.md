# HomeBrewedStars
The Amateur Nucleonics Archive: Case Studies in Backyard Fusion (Personalities, Pitfalls, and Practical Engineering)


# Intro

A collection of in-depth case studies analyzing the independent hobbyists who successfully built operational nuclear fusion reactors at home.
This repository goes beyond the blueprints to explore the "Fusioneer" psychology—the obsessive curiosity, risk tolerance, and relentless grit required to build a star in a garage. By mapping their triumphs and safety pitfalls, this archive extracts actionable lessons in rapid prototyping, extreme resourcefulness, and grassroots innovation.
